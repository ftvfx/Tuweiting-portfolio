About

Tu Wei Ting is one of Taiwan's most prolific VFX Supervisors, with over two decades of experience spanning feature films, premium drama series, and international co-productions across Taiwan, Singapore, Hong Kong, and Japan. A two-time Golden Bell Award winner and multiple Golden Horse and Taipei Film Award nominee, he is widely regarded as a defining voice in Taiwanese visual effects.

Recent credits include Netflix Originals Wave Makers (人選之人—造浪者) — for which he received the 58th Golden Bell Award for Best Visual Effects in Drama — and The Copycat Killer (模倣犯), nominated for the 60th Golden Horse Award for Best Visual Effects. His expanding regional footprint includes the Singapore co-production See You at School at 11PM.

In 2014, Tu founded Filmtailor Studio Co., Ltd. (沸騰映像股份有限公司), which he continues to lead as studio head and hands-on VFX Supervisor. Under his leadership, the studio has delivered visual effects for over 30 productions across major streaming platforms, theatrical releases, and broadcast television.

Prior to founding Filmtailor Studio, Tu served as VFX Supervisor at Chihe Digital Animation, where he led the CG opening cinematic for Final Fantasy XIII-2 — a high-profile commission from Square Enix Japan — and as Post-Production and On-set VFX Supervisor on the Hong Kong theatrical feature Control (控制).

An active practitioner and educator, Tu holds a concurrent appointment as Assistant Professor at Shih Hsin University, where he teaches 3D production, virtual production, and AI-driven filmmaking. He is a pioneer in integrating generative AI into live VFX pipelines, operating a proprietary on-premises AI platform actively deployed in production.
